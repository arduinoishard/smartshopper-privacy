Account & User Data Deletion Request

Overview

At Smart Shopper, we respect your privacy and give you full control over your personal data. You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data at any time directly through the app or by submitting a web request below.

Option 1: In-App Instant Deletion (Recommended)

You can permanently delete your account and all stored shopping data directly inside the Smart Shopper Android application:

  1. Open the Smart Shopper app on your device.
  2. Tap the Profile & Account tab at the bottom right.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account & Data.
  4. Confirm the prompt by tapping Delete Everything.

* In-app deletion is processed immediately and clears all your data from our servers.

Option 2: Web / Email Deletion Request

If you no longer have the app installed or cannot access your device, you may submit a manual deletion request to our team:

Send an email request to:

contact@smartshopper.app

Subject: Smart Shopper Data Deletion Request
Please include your display name or account email associated with Smart Shopper. Manual requests are processed within 7 business days.

Data Types Deleted vs. Retained

Data Category Action Taken Retention Details
Shopping Lists & Items Permanently Deleted Erased immediately from Firebase Firestore database.
User Profile & Display Name Permanently Deleted Erased immediately upon account deletion.
Authentication Credentials Permanently Deleted Firebase Auth account deleted permanently.
Purchase Entitlement Token Retained (Anonymized) Google Play order IDs kept in Google Play Console for legal financial compliance and refund audits.
System Crash Logs Anonymized (90 Days) Non-identifying technical crash reports retained in Firebase Crashlytics for 90 days, then auto-purged.
