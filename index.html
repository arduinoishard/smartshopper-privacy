SmartShopper Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

SmartShopper ("the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information SmartShopper accesses, collects, uses, and stores when you use the app.

Information We Collect

Account Information

SmartShopper may use Google Sign-In to authenticate users. When you sign in with Google, we may receive information provided by Google such as your name, email address, and profile information necessary to provide your account and app functionality.

Location Information

SmartShopper uses location information to provide location-based shopping reminders. Location information may be used to determine when you arrive at a store or other location associated with a shopping item.

Shopping List Information

Information that you enter into SmartShopper, such as shopping items, store assignments, shopping lists, and purchased-item history, may be stored and processed to provide the app's functionality.

How We Use Information

Information is used to:

  • Provide and maintain SmartShopper.
  • Authenticate your account.
  • Provide location-based shopping reminders.
  • Store and display your shopping lists and shopping history.
  • Provide SmartShopper Pro features that you purchase.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the app.

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

SmartShopper may use third-party services, such as Google services, that process information as necessary to provide authentication, payments, or other app functionality. These services process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

Google Play Billing

SmartShopper offers an optional one-time purchase called SmartShopper Pro. Purchases are processed through Google Play. SmartShopper does not receive or store your payment card number or other full payment credentials.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the app. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain account and app information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide SmartShopper and its features.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us using the contact information below. When a valid deletion request is received, we will delete applicable personal information associated with the account, subject to any information that must be retained where required by law.

Children's Privacy

SmartShopper is a general-purpose shopping application and is not directed specifically toward children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
